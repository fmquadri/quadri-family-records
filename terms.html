Terms of Use — Quadri Family Records

Effective 2026-08-20

Quadri Family Records is a personal-use application operated by a single family for its own members. It is not a commercial product, is not offered to the public, and has no users other than the patient whose records it retrieves and his authorized family caregiver.

1. What the application does

With the patient's explicit authorization, the application retrieves the patient's own health records from his healthcare providers' patient-access interfaces (read-only), and stores them on a computer owned and controlled by the family. It performs no other function.

2. What the application never does

  • It never writes to, modifies, or sends anything into any medical record.
  • It never sells, shares, publishes, or discloses health data to any third party.
  • It never uses health data for advertising, marketing, research, analytics, or model training.

3. Authorization and revocation

Access exists only because the patient authorized it through his healthcare provider's own authorization screens. The patient may revoke that access at any time through the provider's portal controls, without notice to anyone. Upon revocation or the patient's request, locally stored copies are deleted.

4. Legal character

The application is an exercise of the patient's individual right of access to his own health information (45 CFR §164.524 and the 21st Century Cures Act). Its operator is a family member acting as the patient's personal representative/caregiver — not a HIPAA covered entity, not a business associate, and not a healthcare provider. The application provides no medical advice; all clinical decisions belong to the patient's treating clinicians.
