Privacy Policy — Quadri Family Records

Effective 2026-08-20

This application belongs to one family and handles the health records of one patient, at his direction. This policy states, completely, what happens to that data.

1. What is collected

Only the patient's own health records (such as lab results, medications, conditions, encounters, immunizations, and clinical documents), retrieved read-only from his healthcare providers' patient-access interfaces after he authorizes each connection himself.

2. Where it lives

On a computer owned and controlled by the family, in local storage, and in a private repository controlled by the family. It is not stored on the application operator's behalf by any third-party service, and the application transmits nothing to any cloud service of its own.

3. Who can see it

The patient and his authorized family caregiver. No one else. No sale of data, no sharing with third parties, no advertising, no analytics, no research use, no training of AI models on the data.

4. Security

Access credentials and tokens are stored in the operating system's credential manager, never in files or logs. Retrieval is read-only by construction — the application registers for no write permissions of any kind.

5. Retention and deletion

Records are kept while the patient wishes his family to maintain them. If he revokes access (which he can do at any time in his providers' portals) or asks for deletion, locally stored copies are deleted.

6. Changes and contact

Any change to this policy takes effect only for data retrieved after the change. Contact: Faisal Quadri (the patient's son and registered caregiver), via the email address on file with the developer program under which this application is registered.

This page exists to satisfy application-registration requirements of health-data access programs (Epic on FHIR; CMS Blue Button 2.0). It contains no patient data.
