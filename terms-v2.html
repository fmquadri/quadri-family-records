Use information — Quadri Family Records v2

Published September 8, 2026.

Quadri Family Records v2 is operated by Faisal Quadri as a personal family-caregiver tool. It collects records only for authorized sources and prepares evidence-linked summaries and reports for authorized audiences.

What a connection permits

The provider's authorization screen controls the granted access. Read-only access can continue through a locally protected credential until revoked, expired or otherwise unavailable. You complete sign-in and consent yourself. No write permissions, chart messages, refill requests or appointment changes are requested by this collector.

Limits of the reports

Reports may contain incomplete source coverage, conflicts, errors or AI-generated inferences. They are not provider-authored records and do not direct treatment changes. Where a report and a source chart disagree, the chart takes precedence. Discuss clinical decisions with the treating care team; this application is not an emergency service.

Data handling and audience

Identified records can be retained in private repositories, hosted services and approved external AI services as described in the accompanying privacy disclosure. Source authorization and report access are separate. Neither a connection nor this notice authorizes disclosure to an additional recipient.

Stopping access

You may revoke the provider connection or ask the operator to stop collection. This stops future access when implemented but does not automatically delete previously collected material. Repository history, backups, external service retention and recipient downloads limit what can be removed. Report withdrawal prevents future portal downloads and does not recall prior copies.

Contact

Contact Faisal Quadri through your existing caregiver contact or the contact associated with this application in the provider's developer program. Ask for the current privacy disclosure and discuss any requested change to sources, recipients or retention before proceeding.
